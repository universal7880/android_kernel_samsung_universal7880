Samsung S6E3FA3 panels on Galaxy A5/A7 (2017)

The panel node referenced by the DSIM lcd_info phandle must identify its
command, dimming and mDNIe tables through compatible:

- "samsung,s6e3fa3-a5y17": Galaxy A5 (2017), AMS520MV
- "samsung,s6e3fa3-a7y17": Galaxy A7 (2017), AMS568MW

The shared driver selects the matching table set before probing the panel.
Existing timing, power and GPIO properties are unchanged.
